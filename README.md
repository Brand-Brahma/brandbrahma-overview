# BrandBrahma

**An agentic AI platform that names, builds, markets and grows a brand from one account.**

▶ **[Open the interactive overview](https://brand-brahma.github.io/brandbrahma-overview/)** · 🌐 **[brandbrahma.com](https://www.brandbrahma.com)**

## Four AI Operating Systems

| OS | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Naming OS** | Name ideas, live domain availability, trademark and company checks, domain appraisal |
| **Branding OS** | Logo studio, brand kit, ads, social posts, landing pages and merch |
| **Marketing OS** | Search visibility (SEO, AEO, GEO), social media, content and paid ads, run from one console |
| **Marketplace OS** | Buy and sell premium domains, with agents that answer offers and match buyers |

## How it works

- **One click, finished result.** Ask for a logo, a brand kit or an audit and get the finished deliverable.
- **You stay in control.** Marketing agents propose a plan, act only on the steps you approve, then show the before and after.
- **Agents in the background.** Some agents work on their own within limits you set, such as answering domain offers or drafting next week's posts.
- **One wallet.** Credits work across every agent, with pay-as-you-go top-ups or Pro and Max plans.
